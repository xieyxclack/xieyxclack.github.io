Tongyi Lab, Alibaba Group

Email: yuexiang.xyx AT alibaba-inc.com

I am a research scientist at Tongyi Lab, Alibaba Group, and a member of the AgentScope-ai team, where we build and maintain open-source projects such as AgentScope and QwenPaw, among others. I received my Master's Degree from Peking University in 2020, advised by Prof. Ying Shen and Prof. Kai Lei.

My research focuses on large language models (LLMs) and agent systems, with the goal of bridging academic advances and real-world applications. As first author or industrial mentor, I have published 50+ papers (2,800+ citations) at top venues such as ICLR, ICML, NeurIPS, VLDB, SIGMOD, KDD, ACL, EMNLP, AAAI, SIGIR, WWW, etc., covering topics spanning LLM-based agents, multi-agent systems, reinforcement learning for LLMs, LLM alignment, federated learning, knowledge graphs, and AutoML.

Google Scholar dblp GitHub

We are building the next generation of real-world agent applications — from research ideas to open-source projects used by thousands. If you are excited about LLM agents, open-source, and publishing at top venues, feel free to drop me an email. Internship and collaboration positions are open.
